Special Characters Guide

  • * Asterisk (Wildcard)
    Means "every" value. For example, * in the Minute field means "every minute."
  • , Comma (List)
    Separates individual values. Example: MON,WED,FRI runs only on those specific days.
  • - Hyphen (Range)
    Defines a range of values. Example: 9-17 in the Hour field means "from 9 AM to 5 PM."
  • / Slash (Step)
    Specifies increments. Example: */15 in minutes means "every 15 minutes" (0, 15, 30, 45).
